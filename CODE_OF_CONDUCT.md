# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in SystemCompass to be safe and productive for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity or operating system preference.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Give actionable, technically grounded feedback and accept it in the same spirit.
- Respect that contributors use their own machines; never ask for or publish another person's logs, registry exports, screenshots or identifiers.
- Keep security reports private until a fix is available.
- Credit sources when a contribution is based on documentation or prior work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately introducing security-reducing settings, hidden data collection or destructive behaviour.
- Pressuring maintainers for support, deadlines or undisclosed commercial terms.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews and any project communication channel.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour through the repository's private reporting channel or to the maintainer via GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently ban participation.
